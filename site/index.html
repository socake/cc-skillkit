Why a marketplace, not loose skills

Portable

One command rebuilds everything on a fresh box. No absolute paths.

Versioned & validated

Semver per plugin; CI checks every skill's frontmatter, naming, and manifest on each push.

One-command lifecycle

Install · update · uninstall · enable · disable through the official claude plugin CLI.

Design principles

The reasoning behind the kit — visible in every skill.

  • Evidence before assertion. Every conclusion carries a pointer — a log line, a metric, a timestamp. No bare guesses.
  • Read-only first. Skills that touch infrastructure diagnose without mutating state; they propose the fix, a human applies it.
  • Progressive disclosure. SKILL.md stays lean; heavy playbooks live one level down and load only when relevant.
  • Vendor-neutral. Hard-won lessons survive as general guidance — useful to anyone, tied to no one's internal stack.
  • Quality over count. A coherent, trustworthy set beats a kitchen-sink dump. CI is the gate.
